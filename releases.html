Release notes

What changed in each version of Vigil. The newest is at the top.

Version 1.2 15 November 2026Latest

  • Longer clips, Wi-Fi only uploads and playback on the camera. Recordings on the camera phone. A new button on the camera screen lists the clips still on that phone and the rolling recording, hour by hour, and plays them there with no need for Drive or a connection.
  • Upload on Wi-Fi only, in the camera's Recording settings. Clips and the daily time-lapse wait on the camera phone until it is on Wi-Fi. Alerts are still sent straight away.
  • Largest clip size, in the same place: 50, 100 or 200 MB. Smaller clips reach Drive sooner and open faster on the viewer.
  • Signing in to Google is now a step in setting up a camera, so it cannot be missed. It can be put off.
  • An upload log in the camera phone's settings, which says what has been sent to Drive and why anything is waiting.
  • An Off choice for the rolling recording. Switching it off frees the space it was using.
  • One clip can now run to 200 MB, which is eight or nine minutes, instead of two minutes. If something is still happening, recording carries on in a new clip with no gap, and the finished clip uploads in the meantime.
  • A clip that is part-way to Drive when the connection drops carries on from where it stopped instead of starting again.
  • The camera screen shows how far an upload has got, and says in plain words what is wrong if one cannot go.
  • The camera screen makes it plain when the phone is not signed in to Google: clips cannot be watched until you sign in.
  • Clips are now sent whenever Vigil is open, not only while the camera is watching, so a clip recorded before you signed in goes up once you do.
  • The camera screen could go on saying it was uploading after an upload had stopped.
  • Vigil could make its folder in Drive twice. Every phone now uses the same one, and an empty spare is removed.
  • A camera left signed out for a long time could fill the phone with clips. Clips that cannot reach Drive are now limited to 8 GB, and the oldest go first.
  • The camera screen could stay on "recording" if a clip failed to save.

Version 1.0 15 November 2026

  • Movement, people, pets and vehicles recognised on the camera phone, with a choice of what counts for each camera.
  • Sound alerts for smoke and carbon monoxide alarms, breaking glass, a baby crying, a dog barking and shouting.
  • Zones, tripwire lines, and alerts for someone staying too long.
  • Arming by timetable, or automatically when you leave home, and a pause while you are home.
  • Night: automatic brightening, a slow-shutter night mode, and the torch on detection.
  • Up to four cameras.
  • Event clips at 1080p with five seconds of lead-in, saved to a Vigil folder in your own Google Drive and deleted after 30 days unless starred.
  • A rolling recording on the camera phone.
  • A 24-hour timeline with filters, a clip player with sharing, and a one-minute time-lapse of each day.
  • Live view over a direct, encrypted link, with near-live pictures through Drive when a direct link cannot be made.
  • Hold to talk, two-way calls and quick phrases.
  • Alerts with a picture, and alerts of your choice that sound on silent.
  • A siren, a flashing torch and a spoken warning in the phone's voice or your own, by hand or automatically.
  • A home-screen widget and a quick-settings tile to arm and disarm.
  • Full-control and view-only roles for each phone signed in to your Google account.
  • Privacy masks, a choice of whether sound is recorded for each camera, and a recording sign to print.
  • Baby monitor, pet cam, and vehicle and parking guard modes.
  • A PIN for the camera phone and a fingerprint lock for the viewer.
  • Alerts if the camera phone is moved, covered, unplugged, overheating or offline.
  • Restarts after a power cut or a reboot and carries on watching.
  • Live view, recordings, alerts and arming from a browser, on this site.
  • Recordings encrypted with your own passphrase.
  • A video stream for a recorder or player on your own Wi-Fi.
  • Webhooks for a home hub.
  • One-line AI descriptions of alerts, with your own key.
  • A notice in the app when a newer version is out.

Checking your download

The file for version 1.2 is called Vigil.apk. It is 67.4 MB (67,370,665 bytes). Its SHA-256 checksum is:

a07441e4e9c4c0707c215542886bbfcf3fc9644760dd1b1af768091d13ffc28b

On Windows, run certutil -hashfile Vigil.apk SHA256 and compare. Every version is signed with the same key, so each one installs over the last and keeps what you have saved.

Older versions are on the GitHub releases page.
