Where your recordings live

With Vigil, clips are ordinary video files in a folder in your own Google Drive, and the camera phone can keep a non-stop recording of its own as well. Vigil has no servers, so the developer never holds your video and there is no cloud plan to pay for. Clips count towards your own Drive storage.

Live video goes directly between your own devices, encrypted, and everything Vigil detects is worked out on the camera phone.

What Vigil does not do

Vigil is an Android app. There is no iPhone app, though the web viewer works in a browser on any device. It is not on Google Play. It cannot share a camera with a different Google account: a household shares one account and gives each phone a role. It is not a monitored alarm and does not call anyone for you. If you need any of those, one of the larger services will suit you better.
