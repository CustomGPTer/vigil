More apps

Vigil has siblings. Each is an Android app made by the same developer, free, with no adverts and nothing to unlock. Each button downloads the latest app file straight from that app's own page on GitHub.

  • Shelfmark

    An ebook reader that sorts your library

    Shelfmark files your books under bookshop headings, so a library of thousands is as easy to browse as a shop. It keeps your place across devices and reads aloud.

    Download ShelfmarkShelfmark website

    Free, with no adverts. Android 10 or later.

  • Grab

    A video downloader without the adverts

    Grab has its own ad-blocking browser. Open a page with a video on it, tap the round Download button, choose a size, and the file is saved to your phone.

    Download GrabGrab website

    Free, with no adverts. Android 10 or later, 64-bit.

  • PDF+

    A PDF reader, editor and scanner

    Read, annotate, fill and sign, edit, scan, convert, compress and redact. PDF+ brings the tools other PDF apps charge for into one free app.

    Download PDF+PDF+ website

    Free, with no adverts. Android 11 or later.

  • Clearcut

    A background remover that works on your phone

    One tap cuts the subject out of a photo, and hand tools tidy the rest. Save it see-through, put something new behind it, or make it a WhatsApp sticker. Your photos are never uploaded.

    Download ClearcutClearcut website

    Free, with no adverts. Android 10 or later, 64-bit.

  • Retag

    A photo metadata editor and remover

    Retag shows the details hidden inside a photo, such as where and when it was taken and on which phone. Change any of them, or clear them before you share. Your photos are never uploaded.

    Download RetagRetag website

    Free, with no adverts. Android 11 or later.

  • BoxTime

    A boxing round timer

    BoxTime rings the bell, warns you ten seconds out and counts you back in, with the screen on or off. Spoken cues, combo callouts and fifteen fight presets are built in.

    Download BoxTimeBoxTime website

    Free, with no adverts. Android 10 or later.

  • Compress

    A video compressor that keeps the picture

    Compress makes the videos on your phone smaller without a difference you can see, at the full picture size unless you choose a smaller one. Start it, turn the screen off, and it tells you when it is done. Your videos are never uploaded.

    Download CompressCompress website

    Free, with no adverts. Android 10 or later, 64-bit.

  • iLock

    An app locker that cannot go online

    iLock puts a PIN, a pattern or your fingerprint in front of the apps you choose. It has no internet permission, so your code, your logs and your photos stay on your phone.

    Download iLockiLock website

    Free, with no adverts. Android 11 or later.

  • AddTxt

    Puts text on photos

    AddTxt adds text to photos with 1,939 fonts, ready-made styles, layers, shapes, stickers and a pen. Style single words, run text along any line, and put the same title block on a batch of photos.

    Download AddTxtAddTxt website

    Free, with no adverts. Android 11 or later.

Installing one

These apps install the same way Vigil does. Chrome may say the file might be harmful. It shows this for app files that do not come from Google Play. Choose Download anyway. Open the downloaded file, allow your browser to install apps if Android asks, and if Google Play Protect steps in, choose More details, then Install anyway.

Each app has its own website with its release notes, privacy policy and terms, and its own place to report a problem.
